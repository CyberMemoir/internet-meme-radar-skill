# Platform Methods

Use `evidence-rubric.md` first. These are platform-specific sampling cues, not scraping instructions.

## Shared capture fields

```yaml
platform: ""
url_or_artifact: ""
published_at: ""
observed_at: ""
caption_or_title: ""
hashtags: []
creator_or_community: ""
visible_engagement: {}
sound_or_music: ""
comment_patterns: []
visual_template: ""
repost_or_original: unknown
commercial_signals: []
notes: ""
```

Look for independent creator reuse, repeated joke structures, audience recognition, remixes, and migration between communities. Discount duplicate uploads, compilation accounts, celebrity-only reach, ads, and stale results.

## TikTok and Douyin

Prioritize sound reuse, format imitation, duet/stitch/reaction behavior, creator diversity, and repeated comments. Treat visible sound-use counts as snapshots. For Douyin, distinguish platform-native spread from reposted Kuaishou, Bilibili, or offline clips. Do not bypass login, signatures, CAPTCHAs, device checks, or risk controls.

## YouTube Shorts

Check publication dates, repeated audio or edit structures, channel diversity, and whether the same format appears in long-form videos or community posts. Do not equate high views on one Short with template adoption.

## Instagram Reels

Check audio pages, remix behavior, caption patterns, creator niches, and whether reuse is public and recent. Separate Reels-native spread from imported TikTok formats.

## Bilibili

Inspect titles, tags, upload dates, remix/edit conventions, danmu, comments, and fandom context. Separate original投稿 from 搬运 and distinguish a site-wide梗 from a single partition or fandom.

## Xiaohongshu / RED

Read `xiaohongshu.md`. Pay special attention to note categories, cover-text imitation, saves versus joke adoption, comment-level phrasing, and commercial seeding.

## Weibo

Check whether a phrase is naturally used, merely discussed, or boosted through entertainment topics and fan coordination. Hot-search visibility alone does not establish durable meme adoption.

## Kuaishou

Track original creator attribution, dialect/regional context, audio reuse, and repost migration. Avoid assuming that later appearances on Douyin reveal the origin.

## X

Follow quote-post and reply conventions, phrase reuse across unrelated accounts, timestamped search results, and community boundaries. Separate organic jokes from coordinated posting or copied engagement bait.

## When access is incomplete

State exactly what could not be observed. Ask the user for 3–10 representative artifacts with visible dates and context rather than asking for a large unsorted dump.
