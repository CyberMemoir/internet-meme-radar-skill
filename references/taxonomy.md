# Meme Taxonomy

Apply one or more labels. Classify the reusable unit—not merely the post containing it.

- **Slang:** word or phrase with community-specific pragmatic meaning.
- **Catchphrase:** repeated quote, sentence, setup, or response pattern.
- **Reaction meme:** reusable response to a situation or emotion.
- **Audio meme:** repeated sound, voice line, song fragment, or remix.
- **Challenge / choreography:** repeated task, gesture, dance, or participation rule.
- **Visual template:** repeated image, framing, pose, object, filter, or scene composition.
- **Editing grammar:** repeated cut rhythm, transition, zoom, subtitle, or reveal structure.
- **Character / persona:** recurring identity, archetype, voice, or creator performance.
- **Ironic / satirical:** depends on parody, bait, anti-humor, or saying the opposite.
- **Linguistic play:** homophone, pinyin, romanization, typo, translation, or spelling joke.
- **Regional / dialect:** depends on a location, dialect, accent, or local community.
- **Fandom / subculture:** requires knowledge of a show, game, idol, sport, profession, or niche.
- **Event / controversy:** tied to news, drama, backlash, scandal, or discourse.
- **Commercial / manufactured:** brand-led, campaign-led, seeded, or optimized for sales.
- **Historical / revived:** recognized mainly as nostalgia or returning in a distinct new wave.
- **Unknown:** accessible evidence cannot identify the reusable unit.

Record the meme's components when useful: `text + audio + visual + interaction`. Different components may have different origins and lifecycles.
