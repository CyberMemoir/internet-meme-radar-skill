# Meme Taxonomy

Use one or more labels:

- Slang: a word or phrase with a community-specific meaning.
- Catchphrase: repeated quote, sentence, or response pattern.
- Reaction meme: used to respond to a situation or emotion.
- Audio meme: repeated sound, song clip, voice line, or remix.
- Dance/challenge: repeated choreography or task format.
- Visual template: repeated image, framing, gesture, outfit, object, filter, or scene composition.
- Editing style: repeated transition, caption style, cut rhythm, zoom, subtitle style, or remix structure.
- Character/persona meme: a repeated identity, role, archetype, or creator persona.
- Irony/satire: meaning depends on sarcasm, parody, bait, or anti-humor.
- Homophone/pun: depends on sound similarity, spelling play, pinyin, romanization, or deliberate misspelling.
- Regional/dialect meme: depends on local dialect, accent, community, or region-specific usage.
- Fandom meme: specific to a show, game, idol, creator, sports team, or subculture.
- Controversy meme: tied to drama, backlash, cancellation, scandal, or discourse.
- Marketing meme: brand-driven, campaign-driven, or commercialized trend.
- Outdated meme: recognized but mostly nostalgic, ironic, or stale.
- Unknown: evidence is insufficient.

Do not force one label. Many memes combine text, sound, visual format, and platform context.
