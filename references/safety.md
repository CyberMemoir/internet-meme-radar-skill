# Safety and Privacy

Explain risky trends without increasing their ability to harm.

## Data minimization

- Use public, relevant evidence and collect only fields needed for the claim.
- Do not reveal private identifiers or infer sensitive traits about private people.
- Paraphrase long captions, comment chains, transcripts, and lyrics; quote only the minimum needed.
- Avoid embedding usernames when aggregate examples are sufficient.

## Risky trend handling

- **Harassment or bullying:** explain meaning and targeting dynamics without directing attention toward a victim.
- **Dangerous challenges:** describe the pattern and documented risk, not replicable steps.
- **Self-harm:** use non-graphic, supportive framing and avoid romanticizing participation.
- **Drugs, weapons, or illegal acts:** omit operational details that enable wrongdoing.
- **Sexual content:** keep the explanation proportionate to the user's context; protect minors and private people.
- **Hate or coded abuse:** decode the usage enough to recognize it without generating promotional variants.
- **Health misinformation:** distinguish the joke from any factual claim and verify consequential claims separately.

When a harmless explanation is possible, preserve cultural meaning, tone, and context rather than replacing the answer with a generic warning.
