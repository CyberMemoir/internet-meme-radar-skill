# Safety Notes

Do not collect or reveal private user information. Do not identify private people unless the user explicitly asks and it is necessary.

Avoid repeating long captions, comment chains, transcripts, or lyrics. Short excerpts are enough when needed.

For risky topics:

- Minors: avoid sexualized detail and do not identify private minors.
- Bullying/harassment: explain the meme without amplifying targeting.
- Dangerous challenges: describe risk at a high level; do not provide steps.
- Drugs/weapons/illegal behavior: avoid operational detail.
- Self-harm: provide safe, supportive framing and avoid graphic details.
- Sexual content: keep explanations non-explicit unless the user's context requires a safety-oriented summary.

When unsure whether a trend is harmful, prioritize a cautious summary and mention that context matters.
