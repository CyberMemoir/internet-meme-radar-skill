# Xiaohongshu Research Method

Use this reference for Xiaohongshu (小红书/RED) notes, comments, hashtags, search terms, visual templates, and platform-specific slang.

## Query variants

Search the exact phrase plus variants commonly used in notes:

- original Chinese, simplified/traditional variants, pinyin, homophones, emoji, and deliberate misspellings;
- `是什么梗`, `什么意思`, `出处`, `原版`, `模板`, `跟拍`, `挑战`, `最近很火`, `评论区`;
- category qualifiers such as beauty, fashion, food, travel, workplace, campus, parenting, fandom, or city name.

## Evidence to capture

- note publication date and whether it is an edit or repost;
- title, body, hashtags, on-image text, audio, and visual composition;
- creator diversity rather than creator count alone;
- repeated phrasing in comments and whether users understand, imitate, or ask about the joke;
- commercial signals: product links, campaign tags, brand seeding, duplicated copy, and giveaway mechanics;
- evidence of migration from Douyin, Bilibili, Weibo, or offline events.

## Platform-specific cautions

- Search order and the recommendation feed are personalized; do not present them as a global chart.
- A high-save tutorial or shopping note may be useful without being a meme.
- Similar cover layouts can reflect category conventions rather than template imitation.
- Brand seeding can create repetition without organic audience adoption.
- Screenshots often omit dates and edit history; label conclusions accordingly.

## Reporting

State whether the signal is note-level, comment-level, category-specific, or platform-wide. If direct access is limited, ask for 3–10 representative note links or screenshots with visible dates, captions, hashtags, and top comments.
